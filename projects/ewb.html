Regioneering — STEM Outreach

Volunteered with Engineers Without Borders on an outreach trip to rural Victorian primary schools, delivering hands-on engineering activities to increase STEM engagement in under-resourced communities.

About the Programme

Engineers Without Borders Australia's Regioneering programme sends university engineering students into rural and regional schools to run interactive STEM sessions. The aim is to broaden students' awareness of engineering as a career and give them hands-on exposure to practical problem-solving — particularly in areas where access to STEM role models is limited.
