Hybrid Motor Sizing

MATLAB-based modelling tools to size a hybrid rocket motor — iterating combustion chamber geometry, oxidiser–fuel ratios, and nozzle parameters against target thrust and specific impulse.

Background

ARES competes in high-powered rocketry competitions that specify target altitudes and impose strict casing diameter and mass constraints. Hitting those targets requires a propulsion system sized precisely for the vehicle — off-the-shelf commercial motors don't give the flexibility needed to optimise performance within the competition rules.

Hybrid motors — which combust a solid fuel grain with a liquid or gaseous oxidiser — offer stop/restart capability, safer handling than solid motors, and more tuning parameters. The challenge is that the combustion physics are complex: regression rate, mixture ratio, and O/F shift throughout the burn, making analytical sizing non-trivial.

Motor assembly / test stand photo

Approach

I developed a set of MATLAB scripts that model the hybrid combustion cycle from first principles, incorporating regression rate empiricism for paraffin-based fuels. The tool iterates on chamber geometry and propellant mass to converge on a design that meets thrust and total impulse targets while staying within mass and dimensional budgets.

  • Oxidiser mass flow modelling based on injector geometry and tank pressure
  • Fuel regression rate calculated using empirical paraffin regression coefficients
  • O/F ratio shift tracked across the burn to predict combustion efficiency vs time
  • Nozzle sizing (throat and exit area) from target chamber pressure and expansion ratio
  • Isp and thrust vs time curves output for trajectory simulation input
